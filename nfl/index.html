Stats, score and clock come from ESPN's live box score; lines come from DraftKings via ESPN. If DraftKings hasn't posted live props, the line shown may be the pregame line, so check your book and edit the line if it differs. Minutes so far count as real information (role, foul trouble, blowout); shooting mostly regresses (per-minute rate is 80% pregame). Live markets carry more vig, so only bet clear edges.

Projections combine each player's opportunities (pass attempts, carries, targets) with his efficiency, shrunk toward position averages, then adjust for game script (Vegas spread), scoring environment (implied team total), opponent defense vs his position and injured teammates' vacated targets/carries. “est.” lines are estimates of where books open; check your book's actual number.
